Agriculture & Button Mushroom Rates

Regulated mandis in Kurukshetra district reported button-mushroom (medium grade) wholesale rates on 26 November 2026. Ladwa APMC modal around ₹20,000 per quintal (₹200/kg); Thanesar APMC lower, around ₹12,000 per quintal (₹120/kg). Average modal across reporting markets near ₹16,000/qtl. These are Agmarknet-derived figures; farm-gate and retail differ.

Sources: MandiPulse (26 Nov), KisanDeals, commoditymarketlive, Shroomy Delights (updated 27 Nov). Tier 2 (secondary citing official data.gov.in / Agmarknet).
