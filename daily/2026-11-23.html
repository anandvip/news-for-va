AI & Global Markets: COP31 collapses, Gemini adds GIFs, and Sarvam prices 105B

The longest UN climate summit, COP31 in Antalya, collapsed without an agreement after EU and island nations rejected a draft text that omitted fossil fuels and finance commitments (x.ai Morning Brief). On Wall Street, US equity futures rebounded Monday morning after Friday's AI-led rout capped a −10% week for the Nasdaq—its worst since 2020.

In AI product updates, Google rolled out GIF generation in Gemini powered by Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (DIT Model Radar) and previewed Semantic governance policies in Agent Gateway to audit proposed agent tool calls against user intent before execution (DIT Model Radar).

In India's compute market, Sarvam's Token Factory priced Sarvam 105B on domestic GPUs at ₹10 per million input tokens and ₹25 for output (₹0.25 cached, pre-GST) (DIT Model Radar), while OpenRouter dropped three inference providers and revoked zero-data-retention on 125 endpoints over data-handling failures (DIT Model Radar).

Agriculture, Fertilizer, Grain Stocks & Button Mushroom Rates

Rabi fertilizer supplies remain above pro-rata demand across India and Haryana, central pool grain stocks stand at 817.62 lakh tonnes, and regulated Haryana mandis are trading white button mushrooms at ₹200–₹240/kg.

₹62,370 Cr Rabi NBS · ₹1,350 DAP

Fertilizer & Haryana Rabi Supply

The Centre approved Rabi 2026–27 Nutrient-Based Subsidy (NBS) rates (+64% YoY) keeping DAP fixed at ₹1,350 per 50 kg bag (Drishti IAS). National Rabi availability exceeds demand (Urea 235.33 LMT vs 183.51 LMT; DAP 71.89 LMT vs 51.38 LMT) (DD News), with Haryana allocated 11.5 lakh MT Urea and 2.70 lakh MT DAP (Global Agriculture).

817.62 Lakh Tonnes Central Pool

FCI Grain Stocks & OMSS(D)

Central pool foodgrain reserves held by FCI stand at 817.62 lakh tonnes (iGrain), more than double the 307.70 lakh tonne buffer norm (MaargX). To cool rice prices, the Food Ministry doubled the 2026–27 OMSS(D) rice sale target to 50 lakh tonnes and mandated weekly trader/miller stock disclosures (The Cavalier).

₹200/kg Thanesar · ₹110/kg Azadpur

Button Mushroom Mandi Rates

Latest Agmarknet-fed arrivals show Thanesar APMC (Kurukshetra) at ₹200/kg modal (₹180–₹220/kg range), Ladwa APMC at ₹240/kg (Nov 18), and Azadpur APMC (Delhi) at ₹110/kg modal (₹80–₹140/kg range), against an all-India wholesale median of ₹155/kg (MushroomBazaar).
